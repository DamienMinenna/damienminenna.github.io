About

I work at Irfu, the CEA institute for fundamental research, in the Department of Accelerators, Cryogenics and Magnetism. I develop ALESIA, a platform that optimises superconducting magnet designs across electromagnetism, mechanics and quench behaviour, and I am the AI expert of my laboratory.

Before joining Irfu, I worked on wave–particle interaction: traveling-wave tubes for satellite telecommunications with Thales and the French space agency (CNES), then electromagnetic pulses in laser–plasma experiments. I hold a PhD in physics.
